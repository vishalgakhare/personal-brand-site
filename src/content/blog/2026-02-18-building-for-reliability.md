pubDate: 2026-02-18

Reliability is rarely a single feature. It is the result of many small design choices:

- Failure-aware APIs and retries with idempotency
- Strong observability from day one
- Safe rollout mechanisms and clear rollback paths
- Operational ownership built into team workflows

In large systems, the fastest path is often not the most durable one. Designing for long-term service health pays off repeatedly.
